Privacy Policy — NeetCompass

Effective date: September 30, 2026

NeetCompass (package id com.neetapp.neet_prep) is a NEET UG exam preparation app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

Account information. To access Full mock, the Bi-weekly mock, or Previous Year Papers, you create an account by verifying your mobile number via a one-time password (OTP), handled by Firebase Authentication. We store your verified phone number as part of your account.

Student profile. When you create an account, we also collect: school name, parent's name, parent's phone number (optional), city, pincode, the class you are currently studying in, and — if you have completed Class 12 — which NEET attempt this is. This is stored against your account and is not shared with anyone outside NeetCompass.

Practice and diagnostic activity. Your diagnostic results, chapter practice attempts, mock results, and mastery/progress tracking are stored only on your own device, in a local database. This data is never uploaded to our servers or to any third party, and we cannot see it.

Subscription and purchase information. If you subscribe to a Pro plan, the purchase is processed by Google Play and verified through RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. We do not see or store your payment card details — only whether your subscription is currently active.

What we do not collect. NeetCompass does not use advertising, and does not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

How We Use Your Information

  • Your phone number is used to verify your identity and let you sign back into your account.
  • Your profile information (school, parent contact, city, pincode, class) is stored to operate your account and, where relevant, to assist you if you contact us for support.
  • Subscription status is used solely to determine whether Pro features are unlocked for your account.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Account and profile information is stored using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud infrastructure), primarily in Google Cloud's Asia-South (Mumbai) region. Practice and diagnostic data stays on your device and is not stored on our servers at all.

Third Parties We Share Data With

We share the minimum data necessary with the following service providers, solely to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — account and profile storage.
  • RevenueCat — subscription/purchase verification, once you subscribe.
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing for subscriptions.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and profile information for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us (see Contact below) — we will delete it within a reasonable time of receiving your request.

Children's Privacy

NeetCompass is intended for students preparing for the NEET examination, who are typically teenagers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without a parent or guardian's involvement. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
